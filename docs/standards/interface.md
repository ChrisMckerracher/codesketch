# Mac application design

Codesketch follows a polished Mac creative application model. The painting is the main content. Toolbars, inspectors, palettes, and playback controls are compact working instruments with a clear hierarchy.

Use the system font, familiar desktop control proportions, neutral surfaces, thin separators, restrained shadows, and a system-blue selection accent. Support light and dark appearance. Reserve color primarily for artwork and paint swatches. Group related commands by function and keep the document title quiet. Label actions in familiar application language: Open, Save, Export, Layers, Notes.

Panels use aligned rows and clear grouping. The canvas sits on a neutral work surface with a small edge and enough surrounding space to see its bounds. Empty documents show a concise in-context hint. Pointer, keyboard, hover, focus, selected, and disabled states must remain clear. Window-like affordances must perform their stated action.

The lead defines and reviews the visual system; Herdr workers implement it. Inspect screenshots at desktop size and both appearance modes. Preserve useful painting behavior and the dependency-free implementation.

References: [Apple macOS design](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-macos), [toolbars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars), and [sidebars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars).
